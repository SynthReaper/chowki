## 1. Executive Summary & The Core Hackathon Dilemma

In large residential university campuses and institutional dormitories (2,500+ students), **sporadic gastrointestinal complaints occur daily** due to exam stress, spicy canteen food, sleep deprivation, and lifestyle variance. 

### The Problem
Traditional reactionary reporting faces two fatal extremes:
1. **Panic & False Alarms:** Overreacting to stochastic noise leads to unnecessary mass kitchen closures, academic panic, and campus-wide disruptions.
2. **Delayed Containment:** Genuine point-source water or foodborne contamination (*S. aureus*, *Norovirus*, *Salmonella*) explodes exponentially before health centers recognize a statistical cluster.

### The CHOWKI Solution
**Project CHOWKI** solves this dilemma using a **Dual-Engine Statistical AI**:
1. **Engine 1: Space-Time Permutation Scan Statistics (STPSS Poisson Monte Carlo $N=999$):** Identifies anomalous spatial clustering along residential hostel corridors with empirical significance ($p < 0.05$).
2. **Engine 2: Multi-Parametric Bayesian Pathogen Profiler:** Calculates pathogen posterior probabilities from incubation delays ($\Delta t$), symptoms, and water telemetry residual chlorine dips ($\text{Cl}_2$).
3. **Cross-Tabulation Odds Ratio Matrix ($OR=14.0, p<0.001$):** Isolates the exact tainted dish (*Mess 2 Palak Paneer*) and separates it from benign meals.
4. **Targeted Micro-Containment:** 1-Click food quarantine, automated RO shock chlorination ($2.0\text{ mg/L}$), and geofenced doorstep ORS dispatch without campus-wide lockdowns.
5. **Zero-Knowledge DPDP Act 2023 Compliance:** Client-side SHA-256 salted tokenization, $k$-anonymity ($k \ge 5$), and Section 8(7) statutory consent shredding.

## 4. Dual-Engine Mathematical Architecture

### Engine 1: Space-Time Permutation Scan Statistics (STPSS)
For zone $z$ across time window $t$:
$$\mu_{z,t} = \frac{C_z \cdot C_t}{C}$$

$$\text{LLR} = c \ln\left(\frac{c}{\mu}\right) + (C - c) \ln\left(\frac{C - c}{C - \mu}\right) \cdot \mathbb{I}(c > \mu)$$

Empirical Monte Carlo significance with $N=999$ permutations:
$$p = \frac{R + 1}{N + 1} = \frac{1 + 1}{999 + 1} = 0.002$$

### Engine 2: Multi-Parametric Bayesian Pathogen Profiler
$$P(\theta_j \mid \mathbf{x}) = \frac{P(\theta_j) \cdot \mathcal{N}(\ln \Delta t \mid \mu_j, \sigma_j^2) \cdot \prod_k P(s_k \mid \theta_j) \cdot W(\text{Cl}_2 \mid \theta_j)}{\sum_{m} P(\theta_m) \cdot \mathcal{N}(\ln \Delta t \mid \mu_m, \sigma_m^2) \cdot \prod_k P(s_k \mid \theta_m) \cdot W(\text{Cl}_2 \mid \theta_m)}$$

### Engine 3: Exposure Cross-Tabulation & Odds Ratio Matrix
$$\text{Odds Ratio} = \frac{a \times d}{b \times c}$$

Standard error and 95% confidence interval:
$$\text{SE}(\ln \text{OR}) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c} + \frac{1}{d}}$$
$$\text{CI}_{95\%} = \left[\exp\left(\ln \text{OR} - 1.96 \cdot \text{SE}\right), \; \exp\left(\ln \text{OR} + 1.96 \cdot \text{SE}\right)\right]$$

## 7. DPDP Act 2023 Statutory Privacy Guarantees

1. **Client-Side SHA-256 Salted Pseudonymization**:
   $$\text{Token} = \text{HMAC-SHA256}(\text{Roll Number}, \text{Salt}_{\text{daily}})$$
   - Raw student identifiers never touch server databases or logs.

2. **Strict $k$-Anonymity Threshold ($k \ge 5$)**:
   - Spatial aggregate alerts and geo-fenced advisories are suppressed unless a cluster contains at least 5 individual reports.

3. **Section 8(7) Statutory Right to Withdraw Consent**:
   - Students can shred their health records, symptom submissions, and tokens instantly with 1 click in the Student Pulse terminal.
