## 🎬 Video Recording Plan & Screen Checklist

When recording a 1-to-3 minute video demonstration of **Project CHOWKI**, follow this sequential checklist:

### 1. Opening & Role Designation (0:00 - 0:25)
- **URL:** `http://localhost:5173`
- **Key Visual Elements:**
  - Luminous header and brand badge: `CHOWKI v1.0.0`.
  - Live synchronized epidemiological advisory ticker: `🚨 LEVEL 2 OUTBREAK ACTIVE` / `🟢 BASELINE SAFE`.
  - The 5 distinct mock persona cards: Grand Jury (`judge`), CMO (`cmo`), Warden (`warden`), Dining HACCP Lead (`mess`), Student (`student`).
  - The **`[ 🚀 60s Grand Jury Tour ]`** button.

### 2. The 60-Second Floating Grand Jury Tour (0:25 - 0:55)
- Click **"60s Grand Jury Tour 🚀"**:
  - Show the floating co-pilot dock at the bottom-right.
  - Advance through Step 1 (Dilemma) $\to$ Step 2 (Dual-Engine Math) $\to$ Step 3 (Causal Investigation) $\to$ Step 4 (1-Click Containment) $\to$ Step 5 (DPDP Privacy).
  - Demonstrate the **`[ — Minimize ]`** toggle to reveal the full live dashboard underneath.

### 3. Dual-Engine Math & Monte Carlo Simulation (0:55 - 1:25)
- **Tab:** `Judge Arena (Simulator)`
- **Key Visual Elements:**
  - Click **`[ 🚨 Trigger Scenario A: Real Outbreak ]`**.
  - Show STPSS Poisson Log-Likelihood Ratio calculation ($LLR = 4.82, p = 0.002$) with $N=999$ Monte Carlo permutations.
  - Show Bayesian Pathogen Profiler attributing **Staphylococcus aureus** ($82\%$) due to rapid 3.5h incubation onset.
  - Click **`[ ☕ Trigger Scenario B: Stochastic Exam Stress ]`** to show how STPSS rejects false alarms ($p = 0.88$).

### 4. Deep Root Cause Isolation & Sensitivity Lab (1:25 - 1:55)
- **Tab:** `Cause Solver (Investigation)`
- **Key Visual Elements:**
  - Show the Exposure Odds Ratio table with **Palak Paneer ($OR=14.0, p<0.001$)** flagged in red.
  - Click on Palak Paneer to expand the **Interactive $2 \times 2$ Epidemiological Contingency Table**.
  - Drag the **Incubation Slider ($\Delta t$)** and **Free Chlorine Slider ($\text{Cl}_2$)** in the Sensitivity Lab to show real-time probability recalibration.

### 5. Multi-Role Consoles (1:55 - 2:30)
- **Hostel Warden Desk (`WardenPanel.jsx`):**
  - Show live resident pulse queue along Floor 3 corridor.
  - Click **`[ Deliver ORS ]`** $\to$ status toggles to `[ 📦 In Transit ]` $\to$ `[ ✓ ORS Delivered ]`.
- **Student Pulse Terminal (`StudentCheckIn.jsx`):**
  - Submit a test check-in and show the real-time 4-step stakeholder alert fan-out (CMO, Warden, Kitchen, DPDP).
  - Show the nearest free ORS dispensary finder and the Section 8(7) consent shredder.
- **Containment Commander (`ContainmentCommander.jsx`):**
  - Execute **1-Click Food Lockdown** and **RO Shock Chlorination ($2.0\text{ mg/L}$)**.
